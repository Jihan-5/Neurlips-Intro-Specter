On (1), one point is factual rather than interpretive. PersonalWAB is not a construct of ours in either respect the comment names. Nothing is injected: the agent recommends over a real user's purchase, rating, and review history, and fails on its own. The label is external: success is whether the recommended item is the one that user actually chose, over a 20-candidate slate of real products. The profile text is synthesized from those behaviors, as we disclosed — but by the benchmark's authors, not by us; we defined neither the failure nor the correctness criterion there. The same holds for the real-field conditions (Food.com Recipes, TravelPlanner-native), where scoring is against the true values of real records with no template bank — and in the one such condition where we lose, we reported the loss unprompted, which is not the behavior of a co-designed harness.

On the conditioning point: a user-specific hallucination requires a stated user constraint by definition. That conditioning is the deployment condition of personalized agents, not an artifact of our setup; our 150 un-injected examples established exactly this, and we reported it as a scope boundary rather than obscuring it.
